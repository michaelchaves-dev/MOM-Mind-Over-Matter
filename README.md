> >> ## What is MOM?
> >>
> >> **MOM (Mind Over Matter)** is a habit-breaking app built on the belief that the mind is the most powerful tool we have. The name carries a double meaning — it's both the acronym for the app's philosophy and a tribute to the mothers (*Maes* in Portuguese) who instilled discipline, love, and a no-nonsense attitude toward bad habits in their children.
> >>
> >> This app was inspired by the Portuguese concept of **"Mau Costume"** — loosely translated as "bad habit" — a phrase that Portuguese mothers would say with equal parts warmth and firm resolve. It wasn't a lecture. It was a reminder. A nudge from someone who loved you enough to call it out.
> >>
> >> ---
> >>
> >> ## 🌿 The Story Behind the Name
> >>
> >> In Portuguese culture, a *Mae* (mother) didn't just raise children — she raised character. When a bad habit crept in, she had a name for it: **"Mau Costume"** (*mow koosh-TOO-meh*). It meant: *"This is a bad habit, and you're better than it."*
> >>
> >> This app is built in that same spirit — not to shame, but to remind. Not to punish, but to empower. MOM asks you to look inward, take ownership, and break the cycles that hold you back.
